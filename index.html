How it works

Coordination, not control.

Dispatch is the visibility and connection layer between restaurants and independent delivery partners. We don't dispatch you — we introduce you.

01

Restaurant signals demand

A restaurant posts a delivery request to riders online in their zone.

02

Riders see opportunity

Online riders in matching zones get notified and choose to accept.

03

Direct contact, off-platform

Coordinate the pickup directly via WhatsApp or a phone call.
