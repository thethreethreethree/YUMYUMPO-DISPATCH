What we are

A discovery platform, not a delivery company.

YUMYUMPO Dispatch is the official driver discovery partner of YUMYUMPO. We help restaurants find independent delivery drivers — and we help drivers get discovered. That's it.

We're not a delivery service. We don't employ drivers, we don't own fleets, we don't run deliveries, and we don't process food payments. Dispatch is simply where independent drivers are visible, verified, and reachable — and where restaurants can build their own trusted go-to list.

Once a restaurant finds a driver they like, the conversation happens directly between them — WhatsApp, a phone call, the way real businesses already work. The discovery is ours. The relationship is yours.
