What we are

A connection layer, not a corporation.

YUMYUMPO Dispatch is the official logistics and rider infrastructure partner of YUMYUMPO. We're built around a single belief: hospitality runs on relationships, not algorithms.

We don't employ riders. We don't own fleets. We don't take a cut of food sales or push aggressive dispatch algorithms onto people who deserve better. Dispatch is a place where independent delivery partners are visible, verified, and reachable — and where restaurants can build their own trusted delivery teams.

Coordination happens off-platform — WhatsApp, calls, the way real businesses already operate. What we provide is the visibility, the trust infrastructure, and the connection.

Decentralized

No central dispatch. No algorithmic control.

Independent

Riders set their own zones and pricing.

Hospitality-first

Designed around the way kitchens actually work.
